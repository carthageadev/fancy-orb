Fancy Orbs

Every orb is a galaxy rendered entirely in a fragment shader — starfield, nebulae, dust lanes, aurora, glass refraction and fresnel rim. No libraries, no assets, no canvas 2D: each frame is pure GPU math from seed to pixel.

Live performance

Two render paths, measured on your GPU: single-pass hero shaders and a batched instanced atlas for small orbs. Offscreen orbs pause automatically.

Cost scales with visible orbs, not mounted ones — hidden orbs skip their fragment pass entirely. The shader also rebalances itself by target size: large orbs get the dual-layer glass refraction plus a chromatic lens, small orbs switch to the single-layer instanced program, and both drop dense star dust below ~200px to kill aliasing. Cap your orb count via CSS content-visibility or lazy mounting if you need hard guarantees; also declare <meta name="color-scheme" content="dark"> to keep scrollbars and form controls in dark mode.
